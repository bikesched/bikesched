# Trademark policy

The code of bikesched is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you may use, modify,
embed and redistribute it, also in commercial and closed-source products. The license covers the
code only. The name **bikesched** and the bikesched logo (`docs/logo.svg`) are trademarks of
Simon Büttner, and the Apache License grants no rights to use them (section 6) beyond describing the
origin of the work.

This policy says how you may use the name and logo. Its goal is that users can tell what is, and
what is not, the original bikesched.

## Allowed without asking

- Using the unmodified library under its name, and redistributing unmodified releases.
- Saying truthfully that your product or service uses, works with, is compatible with, or is
  based on bikesched ("Acme Jobs, built on bikesched").
- Naming integrations by what they integrate with, as long as the name does not suggest they are
  official ("bikesched support for Acme", not "bikesched Acme").
- Writing about bikesched: articles, talks, tutorials, books and courses.
- Showing the logo unmodified next to such a reference, e.g. in a list of technologies you use.

## Needs permission

- Naming a fork, a modified distribution, a product, service, company or domain "bikesched", or a
  name that is easily confused with it.
- Publishing modified code under the Maven group `org.bikesched` or as artifacts named
  `bikesched-*`.
- Using the logo modified, as your product's logo, or in a way that suggests endorsement,
  sponsorship or partnership.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the license, but a fork that is distributed to others needs its own name
and logo. Keep the copyright and license notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`) and say that it is based on
bikesched.

## Questions

Unsure whether your use is fine? Open an issue at <https://github.com/sbuettner/bikesched/issues>
or ask the maintainer.
